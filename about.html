A SHORT BIOGRAPHY

I'm Khushaal Arora, a Delhi-based multidisciplinary creative working across independent music, fashion, video and visual art.

I founded GEEKSKII RECORDS as an independent music imprint and have worked on the practical and visual sides of releases, including distribution, release management, artwork, promotion, mixing and mastering.

Alongside music, I've taken on freelance modelling and content work for homegrown and independent labels. My involvement in music videos has also included appearing on camera, contributing to visual direction and colour grading footage.

Outside commissioned work, I explore tattoo design as a hobby. I'm drawn to ornamental forms, intricate linework, lettering and visual symbolism. I'm also learning more about marketing — particularly positioning, consumer behaviour and how brands build meaning with audiences.
